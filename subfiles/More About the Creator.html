About Me

My journey in coding and technology began at age 14, driven by a deep curiosity and love for problem-solving. I hold a Web Design Certificate from CTS College of Business and Computer Science and continue to expand my knowledge through ongoing courses, including Project Management and Generative AI with LLMs.

Previously, I worked as an Assistant Electronic Engineer at an electronic store, where I developed strong skills in hardware troubleshooting, customer support, and technical problem-solving. These abilities naturally transitioned into my passion for web design and development. I’m particularly proficient in HTML, CSS, and Photoshop and enjoy staying up-to-date with the latest web design trends and tools.

I’m currently working toward my long-term goal of becoming a software engineer, with a strong interest in full-stack development. I thrive on continuous learning, collaboration, and creating high-quality digital experiences through thoughtful design and clean, efficient code.
